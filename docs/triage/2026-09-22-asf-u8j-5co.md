## `nmemo-asf.11` — fragmentation claims verified

Claimed: arxiv-nlp `chatgpt` = 21 rows, `large language models` = 16; ~60-100 fragmented
name-groups per corpus; 26-37% of facts touch a fragmented node.

**Exact match on the examples:** `chatgpt` = **21 rows / 21 distinct types**, `large language
models` = **16 rows / 16 types**.

Fragmented same-name groups per corpus (verified):

| corpus | fragmented groups | rows in groups | worst group | groups with >1 type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| qbio | 103 | 221 | 5 | 103 |
| arxiv-nlp | 81 | 241 | **21** | 81 |
| arxiv-cv | 76 | 189 | 8 | 76 |
| dal-cv | 74 | 179 | 9 | 74 |
| dal-nlp | 67 | 199 | 19 | 67 |

Claim "~60-100 groups per corpus" holds (67-103). **In every group, distinct types == row
count** — each same-name row carries a different `entity_type`, which is the type-fragmentation
signature exactly as `nmemo-asf.5` described.

Facts touching a fragmented node — **claim PARTIALLY overstated**:

| corpus | facts | touching a fragmented node | pct |
|---|---|---|---|
| arxiv-nlp | 2862 | 1062 | **37.1%** |
| dal-nlp | 2612 | 894 | **34.2%** |
| arxiv-cv | 2852 | 707 | 24.8% |
| dal-cv | 2565 | 557 | 21.7% |
| qbio | 6955 | 568 | **8.2%** |

The claimed 26-37% band holds for arxiv-nlp and dal-nlp, but **dal-cv (21.7%) and especially
qbio (8.2%) are below it**. Correct the range to **8-37%, corpus-dependent**.

## Why `asf.11` is IN SCOPE for retrieval, not just hygiene
A 21-way split of `chatgpt` means that entity's facts are divided across 21 nodes. The fusion
improved in doc 46 scores entities by MAX over *their* facts, so fragmentation directly starves
the fact-side signal and splits the target's evidence. On the two corpora where the retrieval
experiments run (dal-cv, dal-nlp) this touches 21.7% and 34.2% of facts. It is a plausible
contributor to the R@10 ~0.20 single-substrate ceiling, though that link is **inference, not
measured** — a merge-then-remeasure would test it.
